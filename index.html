Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Is SkillSwap really free?

add

Yes! We operate purely on a time-bank system. When you teach a skill for an hour, you earn an hour to spend learning a skill from anyone else on the platform. No money is exchanged!

Do I need to be an expert to teach?

add

Not at all. Everyone has something to share. Whether you're an advanced professional or simply enthusiatic about a hobby, there's a learner out there looking for exactly your level of experience.

How are sessions conducted?

add

Most sessions are conducted virtually via third party platforms like Zoom or Google Meet if you and your learning partner prefer.

Can I teach more than one skill?

add

Absolutely! You can list as many skills as you feel comfortable teaching. Your profile can showcase a diverse range of expertise, from technical coding to creative arts or even language tutoring.
